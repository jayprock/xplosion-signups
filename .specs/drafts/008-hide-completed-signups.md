# Idea

After a signup is complete, it should no longer be directly visible on the page. Currently, completed signups remain in the list alongside active ones, forcing users to scroll through old/finished signups to find the current one they need.

Key problems:
- Completed signups clutter the active signup view
- Users must scroll past irrelevant finished entries to find current signups
- No distinction between active and completed signup states in the UI

Possible directions:
- Automatically collapse or hide signups once marked complete
- Move completed signups to a separate "completed" section or archive tab
- Filter view that defaults to showing only active/pending signups
- Toggle or expandable section to review past signups when needed
