# Idea

The current signup flow mixes walk-up song signups together with game signups and lawn mowing signups, making it easy for walk-up song entries to get lost in the noise. We need an update that makes walk-up song signups more prominent and accessible so users can find and complete them without scrolling past unrelated signup types.

Key problems:
- Walk-up song signups are buried among other signup categories (games, lawn mowing)
- Users may not realize walk-up song signup exists or miss it entirely
- No clear visual differentiation between signup types

Possible directions:
- Dedicated walk-up song section or tab at the top of the signup flow
- Visual distinction (icons, colors, or card styling) to separate signup categories
- Quick-access shortcut or prominent CTA specifically for walk-up songs
- Reordering or filtering to surface walk-up song signups
